## [Unreleased]

### Added

- One-line installer for macOS and Linux (`install.sh`) and a `kenwise` command to start, stop, open and update the daemon.
- The page is the approved interface: one ongoing conversation kept live by a single stream of changes that resumes after a dropped connection, work cards under the messages they belong to (one message can be about several pieces of work), a Work sheet, look settings that follow you to every device, notes and history, search and a phone layout.
- A model per role: `EV_MODEL_EV`, `EV_MODEL_REVIEWER`, `EV_MODEL_LEARNER`.
- Watching work on the page. A work card's Watch opens its steps in place (reads and searches folded into one line, the running step with its elapsed time, a step's output on a click). Pop out moves the work beside the chat with its live steps, its draft so far, the model it runs on and a box to tell its agent something (Send waits for the current step, Now interrupts); Pop back in or Escape returns it. Messages to an agent show in the conversation with whether they are still waiting. Held work has Carry on.
- Steering, drafts and Carry on. `POST /api/assistant/tasks/:id/messages` lets the person talk straight to one piece of work's agent (kept in the conversation, so KenWise sees it): by default it reaches the agent once its current step finishes, with `interrupt` at once, with `after` when its turn ends. An agent cannot take a message mid-run (issue 27), so KenWise stops it and resumes the same session with the message, telling it when a step was cut short; `EV_STEER_WAIT_MS` (default 120000) is how long it waits for a step. KenWise's own `message_task` steers the same way. The status record lists messages the agent has not been given yet. `GET /api/assistant/tasks/:id/drafts` lists and opens the agent's unreviewed files, never anything outside its folder or anything that may hold a secret. A step's output opens from the agent's transcript (`/steps/:id/output`, cut in the middle past 64 KB). Work on hold carries on with the `carry_on` command.
- Live steps for running work: KenWise reads each agent's transcript into steps with labels made by code (commands sorted into read, list, search or run), keeps finished steps in their own log, and keeps a status record per piece of work with its "now" line (the newest step as it happens), state, round and model. The page's stream carries steps and status records without resending the whole piece of work, and `GET /api/assistant/tasks/:id/steps` pages back through them. Steps replace the old fixed progress sentences.
- Threads of work: each message is filed into the work it is about by a small model after the turn, and new work waits under "Not grouped yet" until the next organise run (every `EV_REGROUP_EVERY`, default `7d`, or when asked), where a stronger model following `apps/daemon/skills/grouping.md` creates and reshapes threads. Runs can be undone; a message you move by hand stays put. In an open thread, each message can be taken out ("Not this thread", after which an organise run may place it anywhere but back there) or put in another thread; in the whole conversation a thread marker's run ends quietly at the next message that is in no thread. The page lists the threads, opens one across days, and groups search results by thread with the matched words marked.
- Search: results open the conversation at that point, can be limited to one thread, and offer to ask KenWise when plain words find nothing. A renamed piece of work is found by its new name.
- Notes keep their situation: every piece of evidence behind a note carries what was going on when it was seen ("replied in one word to a status update while travelling"), so a preference is never held as a bare rule. Passing facts end on the date you gave ("travelling until Sunday"), and `GET /api/assistant/understanding` gives the "right now" read.
- The portrait: a few sentences on who you are to work with, written from the notes by a model following `apps/daemon/skills/portrait.md` (`EV_MODEL_PORTRAIT`), at most once per `EV_PORTRAIT_EVERY` and only after the notes changed. Each sentence rests on notes, keeps its words while they do not change, and every version is kept. KenWise reads it on every turn as an understanding to weigh, and the grouper reads it too. Agents no longer get the whole profile: KenWise names the notes that bear on each piece of work, and the agent and reviewer get only those, with their situations.
- The calls log and reactions: when KenWise makes a call on its reading of you (kept it short, assumed instead of asking), it records the call and one line of why with its reply, and it saves its readings of your words for a piece of work ("Read as"). Your reactions (That's me or Not quite on a portrait sentence, Right call or the other call on a call, an answer to its question) are saved through `POST /api/assistant/understanding/react` as evidence the learner reads, so they reach the portrait through the notes, never as rules. A sentence you said is not quite right is marked as challenged for KenWise from its next turn.
- The "How I understand you" screen: Understanding and Notes tabs. Understanding shows what is true right now (with how much work is under way), the portrait's sentences (each opens to why I think it, with That's me, Not quite and room for your own words; a sentence you challenged says so until it is rewritten), the calls KenWise made recently to judge, how work should be done for you (Firm when you said it, Usually when relied on, Trying this when a guess) and the one question it would like answered, kept on that screen. Under a reply, "Read as" shows how KenWise read your message for each piece of work; striking one tells KenWise in your name.
- A reaction to a call is shown to KenWise beside that reply from its next turn, and a sentence you challenged is rewritten within the hour after the learner has read your reaction, instead of waiting for `EV_PORTRAIT_EVERY`.
- Model choice: when KenWise starts work it chooses the model, with a first and second choice and a line of why for each, saved with the work and shown when the work is read. It chooses from a guide of the models you can reach: catalogue price and context, the claims of the model atlas shipped in `knowledge/models/` (each marked reported or tested, with its source), and your own results with each model. The reviewer uses a model other than the one that did the work where KenWise named one.
- Routes: every model call goes through the best of your accounts that serve that model (the same model at another connected provider, or another account at the same one): usable first, subscription quota before pay-per-token, then measured speed for the price. An account that runs out mid-task hands over to the next one without losing the work, and a change of route shows as a step of the work. A record of every model call (`model_calls`) measures each route's speed.
- README, docs and project files for open-source use: configuration guide, roadmap, contributing guide, AGENTS.md, security policy, code of conduct, issue and pull request templates, CI.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-13

First public state of the rebuild: inbox per conversation, KenWise as a tool loop with search and read, one SQLite store with one full-text index, OpenClaw agents in docker with a review loop that has no round limit, work that can be held, steered and reopened, memory learnt in the background with said versus guessed and a dated history, forget, and Claude Code session import.
